# Quest map tags (`m` field): sources

Each tag needs two sources (CLAUDE.md sourcing rules). Source 1 is the
arcraiders.wiki Location field (the Quests table, or the quest's own page when the
table cuts off). Source 2 is listed per quest. Researched Oct 18, 2026, patch 2.1.0.

## Dam Battlegrounds (27 quests)

Wiki Location includes Dam Battlegrounds for all of these.

Objectives name Dam Battlegrounds / The Dam directly (MetaForge/ArcTracker quest text):
greasing-her-palms, what-we-left-behind, medical-merchandise, eyes-in-the-sky,
a-symbol-of-unification, the-majors-footlocker, back-on-top,
source-of-the-contamination, our-presence-up-there, a-rising-tide, taking-stock,
the-league

ArcTracker quest page lists Dam Battlegrounds as a map:
a-better-use, water-troubles, paving-the-way, flickering-threat

Walkthrough guide places it on Dam Battlegrounds:
- straight-record, echoes-of-victory-ridge: Shacknews, "Where is Victory Ridge" (on Dam Battlegrounds)
- keeping-the-memory: Dexerto walkthrough
- a-balanced-harvest: Destructoid walkthrough
- untended-garden: GamesFuze walkthrough
- celestes-journals: GamesFuze walkthrough
- broken-monument: Dexerto walkthrough
- dormant-barons: FDayTalk (Baron husks on Dam Battlegrounds, Spaceport, Blue Gate)
- a-new-type-of-plant: GamesFuze walkthrough (Old Battleground, Dam)
- a-toxic-trail: Dexerto walkthrough
- to-the-skies: KeenGamer walkthrough (Gondola Prototype near Red Lakes, Dam)

Several of these also take place on other maps (wiki lists them). Those maps get
tagged in their own passes.

## Buried City (26 quests)

Wiki Location includes Buried City for all of these (wires-crossed: its own page lists
Blue Gate, Spaceport, Buried City).

Objectives name Buried City directly:
greasing-her-palms, what-we-left-behind, medical-merchandise, marked-for-death,
esr-analyzer (A Reveal in Ruins), eyes-in-the-sky, unexpected-initiative, back-on-top,
paving-the-way, a-rising-tide, taking-stock, the-league, wires-crossed

ArcTracker quest page lists Buried City as a map:
a-better-use, market-correction, the-root-of-the-matter, a-warm-place-to-rest,
communication-hideout, digging-up-dirt

Walkthrough guide places it on Buried City:
- eyes-on-the-prize, after-rain-comes, life-of-a-pharmacist, building-a-library,
  line-in-the-sand: Destructoid walkthroughs
- industrial-espionage: ExitLag guide (Gas Station, north Buried City)
- combat-recon: GamesFuze walkthrough

Note: The Root of the Matter's "Research Building" is in Buried City (wiki and
ArcTracker agree), not Dam's Research & Administration building.

## Spaceport (26 quests)

Wiki Location includes Spaceport for 24 of these (the Quests table, or the quest's
own page). The wiki pages for when-everything-falls and empathetic-prototypes didn't
load, so those two use other sources (below).

Objectives name Spaceport directly:
greasing-her-palms, what-we-left-behind, medical-merchandise, eyes-in-the-sky,
back-on-top, switching-the-supply, prescriptions-of-the-past, power-out,
deciphering-the-data, dust-on-the-wires, taking-stock, the-clean-dream,
the-stench-of-corruption, wires-crossed

ArcTracker quest page lists Spaceport as a map (or its quest text names it):
a-better-use, a-wrench-in-the-works, a-lay-of-the-land, on-the-map, turnabout,
waking-the-grid, lost-in-transmission (Control Tower A6), worth-your-salt
(Rocket Assembly)

Walkthrough guide:
- dormant-barons: FDayTalk (Baron husks on Spaceport)
- to-the-skies: KeenGamer (probe and courier scans on Spaceport)

No wiki page, two other sources:
- when-everything-falls: ArcTracker (Spaceport) + Destructoid and KeenGamer walkthroughs
- empathetic-prototypes: ArcTracker (Spaceport) + objectives name the Spaceport Trench
