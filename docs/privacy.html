Speranza Quest Atlas — Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Short version

Speranza Quest Atlas doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or trackers.

Your quest progress

The quests you mark as done are saved only on your device. They're never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Quest data updates

The app may download an updated quest list from our website so it stays current after game patches. That request contains no personal information. Like any web server, the hosting provider may briefly log standard request details such as IP address for security and reliability. We don't use those logs to identify you.

Links to other sites

Wiki and map links open third-party websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
